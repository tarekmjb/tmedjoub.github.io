01 / about

From network operations to project delivery.

My core is Cisco routing & switching and Fortinet security: VLANs, OSPF/BGP, firewall policies, NAT and IPsec VPNs across multisite networks.

At Condor Electronics I became a team leader, running a multisite industrial network with four technicians and two engineers. I also moved its intersite connectivity to Fortinet SD-WAN.

Today I extend on-prem networks into the cloud: FortiGate to Azure VPN Gateway and AWS Transit Gateway over IPsec with BGP, monitored centrally with PRTG. An MBA in project management completes the picture.

I don’t just make it work. I make it resilient, documented and observable.

02 / what i do

How I can help your infrastructure.

As an independent Network & Security Engineer, I audit and strengthen existing infrastructure. Every engagement ends with clear, usable documentation.

svc-01

Network & security audit

Review of switching, routing and firewall configurations (VLAN, ACL, OSPF/BGP), with prioritised findings.

Firewall policy review

FortiGate rule bases, zones & DMZ, segmentation, IPS profiles and hardening. Tighter policy, less exposure.

IPsec / SSL VPN architecture, SD-WAN SLA rules, path steering and dual-ISP failover design.

Site-to-cloud VPN to Azure VPN Gateway and AWS Transit Gateway, BGP route propagation and monitoring.
