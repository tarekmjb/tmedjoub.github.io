LAN Architecture Optimisation

Replaced a flat /16 network (800+ hosts in one broadcast domain) with a 3-tier core/distribution/access design: VLAN segmentation, OSPF, HSRP gateways, ACLs and SNMPv3.

Context & objectives

Design and lab validation in EVE-NG. The topology and configurations describe the architecture; figures are not presented as independently audited production results.

The starting point was a flat network: one /16 subnet with more than 800 hosts in a single broadcast domain, no segmentation, and uplinks that saturated. Any broadcast storm or compromised device could reach everything.

  • Structure the campus into core, distribution and access tiers.
  • Segment by role with VLANs and filter between segments with ACLs.
  • Remove single points of failure for user gateways (HSRP).
  • Make the network observable with SNMPv3 and PRTG.

Implementation

Audit & traffic baseline

Mapped the flat topology with CDP/LLDP, measured broadcast levels and uplink utilisation.

3-tier design

Dual Catalyst 9500 core, one distribution switch per building, access per floor with port-channel uplinks.

VLANs & trunks

802.1Q trunks with explicit allowed-VLAN lists and an unused native VLAN (99). Unused VLANs pruned.

OSPF & HSRP

SVIs on distribution switches, OSPF area 0 towards the core, HSRPv2 per VLAN with interface tracking and sub-second timers.

ACL enforcement

Guest blocked from internal VLANs, IoT allowed only to its servers, management reachable only from the network team.

Monitoring & validation

SNMPv3 to PRTG (utilisation, CPU/memory, STP changes). Validated with a ping matrix across VLANs, captures and STP root checks.

Results

Smaller blast radiusFrom one /16 broadcast domain with 800+ hosts to /24 segments per role.
IsolationGuest and IoT traffic filtered at distribution; no direct path to corporate or server VLANs.
Gateway redundancyHSRP switchover under one second in the lab with 250 / 750 ms timers, checked with continuous ping.
ObservabilitySNMPv3 on every switch, PRTG dashboards and STP topology-change alerts.
