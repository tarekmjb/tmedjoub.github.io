## About Me

I am a Network & Security Engineer with hands-on experience operating in critical enterprise environments where uptime isn't a goal — it's a requirement. My work sits at the intersection of infrastructure reliability, secure network design, and high-availability architecture, with a strong command of Fortinet ecosystems, SD-WAN migration, firewall policy management, VPN connectivity, and proactive infrastructure supervision.

What sets me apart is the combination of two complementary skillsets: deep technical execution and strategic project thinking. Alongside my engineering background, I am currently completing a Master's in Management & IT Project Governance, which gives me the ability to translate complex infrastructure challenges into structured, business-aligned delivery plans — a rare bridge that most purely technical profiles don't offer.

I have designed and secured enterprise LAN/WAN architectures, contributed to SD-WAN migrations at production scale, built redundancy solutions across geographically distributed sites, and handled N3-level incident resolution in time-sensitive operational contexts. I work equally well autonomously and in collaborative, cross-functional teams — bringing both the technical rigor and the communication clarity that modern IT environments demand.

My long-term goal is to contribute to organizations that take infrastructure security seriously — building networks that are not only fast and reliable, but resilient, observable, and built to evolve with the threat landscape.

## Featured Projects

### SD-WAN Enterprise Migration
> Migrated a multi-site enterprise network from legacy WAN topology to a centrally managed SD-WAN architecture, significantly improving inter-site connectivity, routing efficiency and operational visibility.

**Architecture:** Multi-site SD-WAN fabric with FortiManager centralized control plane, dynamic path selection and integrated VPN tunneling across branches.

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| WAN Fabric | SD-WAN (Fortinet) |
| Overlay | VPN IPsec tunnels |
| Management | FortiManager |
| Routing | BGP / OSPF |

**Impact:** Reduced WAN complexity, improved path reliability and enabled centralized policy enforcement across all sites.

---

### LAN Architecture Optimization
> Redesigned the core switching architecture across distribution and access layers to eliminate bottlenecks, reduce broadcast domains and enforce network segmentation policies.

**Architecture:** Three-tier LAN model (core / distribution / access) with OSPF-based routing, VLAN segmentation, ACL enforcement and SNMP-based monitoring.

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| Switching | VLAN, STP, VTP |
| Routing | OSPF, DHCP |
| Security | ACL policies |
| Monitoring | SNMP, OpManager |

**Impact:** Improved network performance, enforced security zones and reduced fault propagation across the production environment.

---

| Component | Technology |

**Impact:** Eliminated single points of failure across remote sites and established automated failover for business continuity.

| Component | Technology |

### Hybrid Cloud Connectivity Lab — Azure & AWS

| Component | Technology |

| Routing | BGP across all environments |
| Monitoring | PRTG, Azure Monitor |

**Impact:** Produced a replicable hybrid connectivity blueprint aligned with Aviatrix multicloud principles — directly supporting CCNP and Multicloud Network Associate knowledge applied in a practical lab context.

`FortiGate` `Azure` `AWS` `BGP` `IPsec` `Aviatrix` `PRTG` `Hybrid Infrastructure`
